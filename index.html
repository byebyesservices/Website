Equipment & Customer FAQ

Hardware Specifications & On-Site Requirements

Do you need a water hookup on-site?

Yes—as a 100% mobile service, ByeByes Services connects directly to your standard outdoor residential water spigot on-site. We verify water flow (GPM) and drainage during our pre-wash inspection.

What commercial pressure washing hardware do you run?

We run a DeWalt 3,400 PSI / 2.5 GPM commercial gas pressure washer paired with a 15-inch rotary surface cleaner (to eliminate wand zebra striping) and a General Pump 100775 downstream chemical injector.

Is pressure washing safe for my car's paint and clear coat?

Absolutely. For mobile auto detailing, we step pressure down to a clear-coat-safe 1,200 – 1,900 PSI using dedicated 1/4-inch quick-connect #4.0 and #4.5 low-pressure orifice nozzles alongside a pH-balanced snow foam pre-soak and two-bucket microfiber wash.

How can I get the fastest price quote?

Call or text (657) 504-6183 with a couple of photos of your driveway or vehicle! We can also measure driveway square footage via satellite or on-site inspection.
